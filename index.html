About Law

Born and raised in Plaquemine, Louisiana, Law Lucas recorded his first song at ten, inspired by his father's DJ sets and the mixtape culture around him. He came up performing with Bow City Boys and JLT3, whose "Candy Girl" made noise across the region.

He went solo as Law Lucas in 2019 and has since released Jet Skis & Palm Trees, The LAW Lucas Story and a run of singles that mix Southern roots, zydeco and feel-good energy. "Trail Ride" took over Southern clubs, and "Kuntry Gurl Zydeco" keeps the momentum going. In 2026 he joined Z-Connections for management.
